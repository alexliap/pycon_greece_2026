# Meaning becomes arithmetic

If *where* a word sits carries meaning, then **directions** carry meaning too.

The step from `man` to `woman` is a direction. Take that same step starting from
`king`, and you land on `queen`. You can literally add and subtract words.

```python
king - man + woman  ==  queen
```

This was the result that made people sit up in 2013, long before ChatGPT.
Nobody programmed the concept of gender, or of capital cities. It fell out of
counting which words appear near which other words.
