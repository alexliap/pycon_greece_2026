# Meaning becomes arithmetic

If *where* a word sits carries meaning, then **directions** carry meaning too.

The step from `greece` to `athens` is a direction: country to capital. Take
that same step starting from `france`, and you land on `paris`. You can
literally add and subtract words.

```python
athens - greece + france  ==  paris
```

This was the result that made people sit up in 2013, long before ChatGPT.
Nobody programmed the concept of capital cities. It fell out of counting
which words appear near which other words.
